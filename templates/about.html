Methodology & Technical Architecture

Open-access research documentation. No sign-up or authentication is required to review this methodology.

1. Supervised Machine Learning Pipeline

HealthRecom deploys dual classification architectures evaluated against a standardized 132-symptom feature space derived from clinical records:

  • Random Forest Classifier (rf.pkl): 100-tree ensemble capable of multi-class probability scoring via predict_proba().
  • Linear Support Vector Classifier (svc.pkl): Linear hyperplane separating 41 discrete condition classes. It deliberately omits uncalibrated probabilities to preserve diagnostic integrity.

2. Symptom Feature Vectorization

Client-selected symptoms are dynamically mapped to a 132-dimensional binary vector [0, 1] matching the precise feature order established during training (symptoms_list.json). Cumulative symptom severity is calculated through empirical clinical weights (Symptom-severity.csv).

3. Knowledge-Based Recommendation Engine

Predictions are linked deterministically to 5 relational knowledge bases without fabricating collaborative or deep learning assertions:

  • Condition Descriptions: description.csv
  • General Medications (Educational only): medications.csv
  • Clinical Precautions: precautions_df.csv
  • Dietary Guidelines: diets.csv
  • Lifestyle & Workouts: workout_df.csv

4. Theoretical Architecture & Research Roadmap

In adherence to strict academic honesty, future extensions remain modular and clearly separated from active V1 inference:

  • Content-Based Filtering: TF-IDF vectorization & Cosine Similarity across clinical textual corpora.
  • Collaborative Filtering: Matrix Factorization (Truncated SVD) on anonymized perceived recovery utility matrices.
  • Context-Aware Optimization: Multi-dimensional vital adjustments incorporating historical risk profiles.
